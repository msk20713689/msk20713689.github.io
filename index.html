About

I'm an accomplished marketing executive with more than 20 years of experience across marketing, product management, and general management. Along the way, I've been fortunate to help clients including Ameriprise, McKesson, HSBC, ABN AMRO, Pfizer, and Intel achieve their business goals.

I've been successful in companies large and small — software, hardware, and services — across more than a dozen countries. Early in my career, I helped start ECI Technology, a pioneer in electrochemical test equipment and a Deloitte "Fast 100" award winner.

I love to learn: I hold a degree in Chemistry from Binghamton University, a Computer Science technical diploma and Master's-level coursework in Computer Science from Hofstra University, and an MBA from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

Originally from Long Island, NY, I've made stops in Chapel Hill and San Diego, but now call suburban Philadelphia home, where I live with my wife Rachel and our two daughters. In my limited spare time, I enjoy cooking, gardening, camping, and hiking.
